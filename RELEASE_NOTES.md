iOS-style launcher for Android 17 (works on Android 12+).

**Install:** download `iOSLauncher-*.apk` below, open it on your phone (allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser/file manager), then press Home and choose **iOS Launcher** as the default home app.

### Features
- Exact iOS app-icon shape (continuous-corner squircle) for every icon, with any installed icon pack (ADW / Nova / Apex / Lawnchair formats)
- Custom icon per app: your own picture or any icon from an installed pack
- iOS 26-style unlock animation (staggered spring fly-in), apps shrink back into their icon when closed
- App Library with automatic categories; 3D tile carousel search with live filtering and suggestions
- Liquid Glass material (refraction, blur, shine — all adjustable) on dock, folders, menus, App Library
- Folders resizable by grid cells, widgets, grid up to 8 × 8, free placement (no auto-fill)
- Dock on/off or hidden on page 1 only (page 1 then gains an extra row + glass search button)
- Full screen with clock and battery / Wi-Fi / signal indicator on page 1 (adapted from Duo Launcher, MIT)
- Tilt parallax, iOS-style long-press menus, jiggle edit mode with drag & drop

### Notes
- For real wallpaper glass on Android 14+, allow *Settings → Liquid Glass → Allow Wallpaper Access* (All files access).
- The APK is signed with a public sideload key — fine for personal installs, not for Play Store.
